# Feedback form (tester)

> Fill it in after the four fights. Short answers are fine. There are no wrong answers: if something didn't make sense,
> say so. The host can paste these questions into a Google Form or a Discord message; keep the numbers so the answers map to
> the write-up. The tag after each question is the `10` §2 test it feeds (hosts only; leave the tags out of a pasted form).

**Name or tester number:** ______  **Played on:** PC / laptop, mouse / trackpad  **Do you play sports or fighting games?** ______

## A. The catch

1. In fight 2 or 3, your Wrestler caught a kick and took the Kicker down. In your own words, **why did that work?** *(Feel, test 1)*
2. How did you know the kick was coming? *(Readability, "the opening")*
3. When it worked, how did it feel? (1 = nothing special, 5 = loved it) **1 2 3 4 5** What made it feel that way? *(Feel)*
4. Did you ever press **Catch the kick!** and nothing happened? What do you think went wrong? *(Timing, Fairness)*

## B. When things failed

5. Did a takedown ever fail? **Why do you think it failed?** (For example: he sprawled, he kneed you on the way in, you were
   too far away, your fighter was tired.) *(Punishment readability, test 4)*
6. Did the feed (the text on the right) help you understand what happened? **Never / Sometimes / Often** *(Fairness: "the log explains why")*
7. When your fighter ignored a call ("nah"), did you understand why? **Yes / Sometimes / No** *(Readability)*

## C. Reading the fight

8. During a fight, could you tell: (tick the ones that were easy)
   ☐ how far apart they were (the range) ☐ who was on top or against the fence (the position)
   ☐ what each fighter was doing right now ☐ when there was a chance to make a call
   ☐ how hurt each fighter was (the health bars) *(Readability)*
9. Without any calls from you, did your fighter still fight sensibly? **Yes / Mostly / No** *(Autonomy)*
10. Did the fights feel fair, or did the Kicker or Boxer seem to cheat or get lucky? *(Fairness)*

## D. Speed

11. Fight 2 was played at two speeds. Which felt better? **The first / The second / No difference** Why? *(D91)*
12. Did you use slow motion (Space)? **Never / Once or twice / A lot** Was it useful? *(Slow motion)*
13. Did anything feel too fast to react to, or too slow and boring? *(D91)*

## E. Overall

14. **Do you want to play another fight?** **Yes / Maybe / No** *(Fun, test 5)*
15. If this were a full game (create a fighter, train them, take them through a career), would you play it? **Yes / Maybe / No** *(Fun)*
16. The best moment, and the most confusing moment: ______
17. One thing you'd change: ______

## F. Getting hurt and finishes

18. When a fight was stopped early (a knockout or the referee stepping in), **did you see it coming?** **Yes / Sometimes / No / It
    never happened** Did the stoppage feel fair? *(Health and the referee, D120)*
19. When your Wrestler was on top, did you call **Ground and pound!**? **Never / Once or twice / A lot** Did it seem to do
    anything? *(Ground and pound, D121)*
20. Did fights end too often before the final bell, too rarely, or about right? **Too often / About right / Too rarely** *(D120's "about a third")*
