# ui/ — interface

HUD, menus, minigame UI and themes (docs/06-fight-hud.md). Presentation only: no game rules here.

- `fight_hud.gd`: HUD v0 for the fight screen (docs/06 §6): top bar, plan panel, opponent corner bubble, feed, voice meter, call bar, and the dialog in the middle (bell, result, menu, towel).
- `fight_feed.gd`: the feed with its All / Key moments / Explanations filter. `voice_meter.gd`: the voice bar with the garbled zone.
- `title_screen.gd`: the title screen the game opens on (docs/03 §10.2, D302): Continue, New career, Load career, Quick fight, Quit. `new_career_screen.gd`: a new career (your name, your coach background and specialty (§11.1, D311), his name, nickname, hometown, stance, background; Roll; *Play his childhood*). `childhood_screen.gd`: his childhood (§11.2, D312): three scenes, a choice in each, then Who he became. `gym_screen.gd`: where you coach and the walk-in (§11.3, D313): three gyms in his hometown, then the morning he walks in. `load_screen.gd`: the three save slots. `tip_card.gd`: the tutorial's tip cards, waiting for Got it, and the Coach's Notebook (§11.6, D316). `career_hub.gd`: the career hub between fights (docs/03 §10.3, D303): his card and stats, This week (the plan, Run this week, Advance), his fights and the calendar; fight offers as cards to take or turn down, Turn pro and Go to camp (§10.4, D304).
- `fight_setup.gd`: the screen before a fight (Quick fight on the title), as a tale of the tape (docs/06 §6.7, D172): any two fighters with ◀ ▶, their scouting cards and the keys to the fight, then the options (speed, difficulty, cue toggles, rules, seed). `tale_of_the_tape.gd`: the tape in its middle (frames and headline stats face to face, the opponent's scouted). `rule_slots.gd`: the two rule slots and the Drilled slider, used there and between rounds.
- `camp_planner.gd`: the training camp planner (docs/02 §3.6, D134; laid out as the camp week HUD, §3.10, D170), from the setup's "Training camp first": plan each week, see its forecast, run the camp, then fight with the trained fighter.
- The camp week HUD's parts (docs/02 §3.10, D170): `session_tile.gd` (a slot's tile, coloured by the session's kind), `fatigue_line.gd` (the week's fatigue under the tiles), `stat_bars.gd` (the stats as bars), `camp_countdown.gd` (the weeks counting down to the fight). `display_font.gd`: the display font for headings and big numbers (`fonts/`, SIL Open Font License).
- `fighter_portrait.gd`: a fighter drawn as himself off the fight screen (docs/06 §6.11, D431): the skinned body with his look, in his stance and breathing, full body or head and chest, with the body figure as a small hurt badge. Used by the hub, fight night card, tale of the tape, result and new-career screens.
- `world_screen.gd`: the world pages (docs/06 §6.12, D432): his division, the rankings, the promotions and the history as one full-screen page with tabs over the hub. With `man_card.gd` (a man's card at the side, with his portrait), `belt_timeline.gd` (a belt's reigns across the years) and `promotion_badge.gd` (a promotion's badge drawn in code).
- `fight_poster.gd`: a fight on the hub as a poster (docs/06 §6.13, D433): an offer or the booked fight, both men face to face, the card in its promotion's colour, ribbons, the countdown. `calendar_icon.gd`: the hub calendar's icons, drawn in code.
- `result_card.gd`: the result screen (docs/06 §6.9, D173): the verdict, both figures, the fight sheet, the judges' cards and your corner's grade.
- `fight_night_card.gd`: the fight night card (docs/02 §3.11, D171), opened over the planner when the camp is over.
- `mitt_work_screen.gd`: the mitt work minigame (docs/02 §3.9, D137), opened by the planner for each Boxing drills session ticked to Play; the judging is `sim/world/mitt_work.gd`.
- `film_study_screen.gd`: the film study minigame (docs/03 §13.4, D410), opened by the planner for each Film study session ticked to Play: the tape in a 3D view of its own, Space to mark, his habit named from three; the judging is `sim/world/film_study.gd`.
- `sprawl_reflex_screen.gd`: the sprawl reflex minigame (docs/03 §13.5, D411), opened by the planner for each Sprawl drills session ticked to Play: the drill in a 3D view of its own (`ArenaView.drill`: no move names, range tag or referee), Space to sprawl; the judging is `sim/world/sprawl_reflex.gd`.
- `debug_overlay.gd`: F3 on the fight screen, the raw numbers behind what's on screen.
- `debug_console.gd`: developer console (toggle with the ` key). "Run test fight" runs `game/sim_check.gd` and prints pass/fail; the fight on screen is logged here with every odds breakdown. Lines are formatted by `fight_log_formatter.gd`.
- `background_icon.gd`: a picture for each of the 12 backgrounds and the five gym kinds, drawn in code (docs/06 §6.14, D434): the new-career tiles and the gym cards.
- `off_cage.gd`: one look for the screens off the fight (docs/06 §6.15, D435): the see-through backing over the dimmed hall, the dark buttons edged in red and the theme every off-fight screen carries.
- `verdict_banner.gd`: the verdict read out over the cage before the result's sheet (docs/06 §6.16, D436): how and when, the judges one at a time, the winner.
